Fitness with Dina — Health & Safety Agreement

Before starting, consult your doctor. If you have a medical condition, injury, symptoms, are pregnant or recovering from childbirth, or are unsure whether exercise is safe for you, obtain medical clearance before participating. Always follow your healthcare professional’s advice.

Exercise carries risks, including serious injury. I voluntarily accept the inherent risks of participating and take responsibility for choosing activities appropriate to my abilities, using equipment safely, and following any medical restrictions. I will stop exercising if I experience pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, unusual breathlessness, or feel unwell, and seek appropriate medical help.
